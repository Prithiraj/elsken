## Opening hours

A Restaurant Guru listing updated in **July 2026** reports:

- Monday: closed
- Tuesday: 10:00–18:00
- Wednesday: 10:00–18:00
- Thursday: 10:00–18:00
- Friday: 10:00–18:00
- Saturday: 10:00–18:00
- Sunday: 10:00–18:00

Source: [Restaurant Guru](https://de.restaurantguru.com/Cafe-Elsken-Berlin).

### Conflict noted

An official Instagram post from **3 June 2025** announced Friday/Saturday/Sunday opening until 19:00. Because the directory hours are substantially newer and show 18:00 across all open days, the prototype uses 10:00–18:00 and tells visitors to recheck before travel.

Historical Instagram result: [“FROM NOW ON !!! Friday Saturday Sunday Open till 19:00...”](https://www.instagram.com/p/DKcUXX1svnU/).

**Launch action:** verify hours directly with the business/Google Business Profile immediately before commercial launch.

## Customer motivation / social proof

Current directory research reports a **Google rating around 4.7/5**. Review themes repeatedly include:

- friendly staff / warm service;
- cozy atmosphere;
- feeling welcome / like a regular;
- homemade/fresh food;
- music/records;
- shakshuka;
- outdoor seating / sunny exterior;
- fair or attractive prices.

Sources:

- [Restaurant Guru](https://de.restaurantguru.com/Cafe-Elsken-Berlin)
- [Coool Cafe — Elsken](https://coool.cafe/cafe/elsken-berlin)

The website converts these recurring themes into design/copy direction instead of reproducing customer quotes as commercial testimonials.

### Rating/count handling

Review counts differed between indexed snapshots (for example, low 200s on Restaurant Guru versus other aggregators). The implementation therefore shows only **4.7** with a research-date caveat and does not hard-code a review count.

## Location and space

Public sources consistently place Elsken at Kiehlufer 75 in Neukölln. Restaurant Guru lists outdoor seating; indexed reviews describe sitting outside and a sunny atmosphere.

Source: [Restaurant Guru](https://de.restaurantguru.com/Cafe-Elsken-Berlin).

The user-provided Google Maps listing confirms the exact business location and was used as the initial research target.

## History

A hospitality trade article dated **11 January 2021** reports that Café Elsken had recently taken over the former Fishtank café at Kiehlufer 75 and identified Dutch-born Berlin resident Florent Affourtit as the operator at that time.

Source: [Meininger/fizzz — Top 5 Openings aus Berlin](https://www.meininger.de/gastronomie/top-5-openings-aus-berlin).

A neighbourhood listing says “Neu geöffnet in Nov. 2020” and “Kiez Café von Florent aus der Harzerstraße.”

Source: [nebenan.de listing](https://nebenan.de/places/businesses/52461).

### Current owner caveat

No sufficiently authoritative current 2026 ownership record was found during public research. Therefore the website does **not** claim that the 2020/21 operator is still the current owner.

## Nearby alternatives / competitive context

The research pass surfaced several nearby café/brunch alternatives, including Nah am Wasser and Pêle-Mêle, as well as other Neukölln breakfast/coffee businesses.

The competitive design conclusion is not based on claiming superior products; it is based on Elsken’s documented combination of:

- small/cozy positioning;
- homemade lunch/cakes;
- specialty coffee;
- Kiehlufer address;
- recurring review themes of personal welcome;
- real participation in neighbourhood/cultural events.

This makes “warm Kiez editorial” a stronger direction than a generic premium brunch aesthetic.

## Facts intentionally not used

The following were omitted because evidence was weak, conflicting, stale, or aggregator-only:

- reservations;
- delivery;
- exact current prices;
- current individual dishes;
- pet policy;
- parking claims;
- alcohol/cocktail range;
- Wi-Fi promise;
- current owner identity;
- employee names;
- seating capacity;
- email address.
